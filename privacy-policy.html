ParkSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

ParkSnap ("the App", "we", "us") is a parking-tracking app. This page explains what information the App collects, why, and how it is used. ParkSnap is built to work fully offline with no account required - the data described below is only collected for the specific features you actively choose to use.

1. Information the App collects

  • Parking photo and GPS location. When you snap a photo of your parking spot, the App stores that photo and your device's GPS coordinates at that moment, on your device, so it can show you directions back to your car. This data stays local to your device unless you turn on optional cloud sync (see below).
  • Parking session details. Vehicle name, parking duration, paid/private status, and optional lot/spot number you enter - stored locally, and in our cloud database only if cloud sync is enabled.
  • Account information (optional). If you choose to sign in (Google or email/password) to enable cloud sync, we store your email address and an account identifier through our authentication provider (Supabase, see below) to back up and restore your parking history across devices.
  • Nearby-store lookups. If you're in an active paid parking session, the App sends your current GPS coordinates to our server, which queries Google's Places API for nearby stores and returns names/addresses. We do not store these coordinates beyond the single request.
  • Anonymous usage analytics. The App logs basic in-app events (e.g. app opened, parking session started/ended, currency changed) tagged with a randomly generated device identifier (not tied to your name or device hardware ID) and, if signed in, your account identifier. This helps us understand which features are used so we can improve the App. No advertising or tracking SDKs are involved.

2. Information the App does NOT collect

ParkSnap does not access your contacts, photos outside the ones you take in-app, browsing history, or any data unrelated to the features above. The App does not sell your data, and does not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-party services we use

  • Supabase - authentication and cloud database, used only if you enable cloud sync or sign in. Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google Places API - used server-side to power the "nearby stores" feature. Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Cloud Vision API - used server-side for optional text recognition (OCR) if that feature reads text from a photo. Google's privacy policy.

Future versions of ParkSnap may include advertising (e.g. Google AdMob banner ads). If so, this policy will be updated in advance to disclose the ad network and any additional data it collects.

4. Your choices

  • You can use ParkSnap fully offline, with no account and no cloud sync - in that mode, your parking data never leaves your device.
  • You can turn cloud sync on or off at any time in Settings.
  • You can delete your parking history and account data by contacting us at the email below.

5. Data security

Cloud-stored data is protected with row-level security so that only your signed-in account can access your own records. We take reasonable measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

6. Children's privacy

ParkSnap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at kbrahmayya@medianet-home.de.
